Featured Project

Rethinking the Normal Curve in Psychological Research
Statistical assumptions · psychometrics · philosophy of science
Challenges a common but often overlooked assumption in data analysis—that human behavior follows a normal (Gaussian) distribution. In practice, many real-world behaviors (e.g., user engagement, performance, decision-making) are skewed, clustered, or irregular. When standard statistical methods assume normality, this can lead to misleading conclusions—especially in widely used approaches like regression, A/B testing analysis, and predictive modeling. This work highlights how these hidden assumptions can distort insights and decision-making, and emphasizes the importance of choosing models that better reflect real-world data patterns. By aligning analytical methods with how people actually behave, organizations can make more accurate, reliable decisions.

Impact: Helps teams identify hidden risks in their analytics workflows, improve model reliability, and build data practices that better reflect real-world behavior.

Status: Under review at General Review of Psychology.

Selected Projects

Power Analysis & Research Design
Monte Carlo simulation · survey methods · statistical modeling
Built simulation frameworks to evaluate statistical power and design tradeoffs in survey-based research, helping identify when data is insufficient to support reliable conclusions. Through a large-scale review, found that ~50% of mediation analyses in organizational research are underpowered—highlighting a significant risk of false or misleading insights in common analytics workflows. Developed practical sample size guidelines across model types to improve reliability and decision confidence.

Impact: Helps teams design more reliable studies, reduce false positives, and make better data-driven decisions with fewer wasted resources.

Status: Under review at PLOS ONE.
Workforce & Immigration Research
Qualitative research · interviews · global talent strategy
Problem: Organizations struggle to support global talent when immigration uncertainty affects employee experience, retention, and performance.

Approach: Conducted in-depth interviews with employees navigating employment-based immigration processes, analyzing how visa delays shape motivation, career decisions, and workplace engagement.

Insight: Immigration uncertainty introduces hidden organizational risks—impacting retention, long-term commitment, and perceived career stability.

Impact: Informs more inclusive talent strategies, helping organizations better support international employees and reduce attrition risk.
Cannabis & Leadership Experiments
Experimental design · behavioral science · leadership
Problem: Limited understanding of how substance use affects leadership communication and decision-making in modern workplaces.

Approach: Designed and conducted controlled experiments to evaluate how cannabis use influences leadership behaviors such as communication clarity, charisma, and judgment. Led end-to-end execution and mentored 20+ researchers.

Insight: Behavioral changes under altered cognitive states can meaningfully impact leadership effectiveness, especially in communication-heavy roles.

Impact: Provides evidence-based insights into behavioral risks and leadership performance, informing workplace policy and leadership development.
Leadership & Sustainability (CSR / ESG)
Organizational behavior · sustainability · operations
Problem: Organizations invest in sustainability initiatives, but lack clarity on how leadership behaviors translate into measurable operational outcomes.

Approach: Analyzed leadership practices within a large food manufacturing organization to evaluate their impact on waste reduction and operational efficiency.

Impact: Supports data-driven ESG initiatives by connecting leadership practices to measurable performance improvements.
