Featured Project

Rethinking the Normal Curve in Data Analysis
Statistical assumptions · behavioral data · decision risk
Problem: Many analytics workflows assume human behavior follows a normal (Gaussian) distribution—even when real-world data does not.

Approach: Analyzed how violations of distributional assumptions affect widely used methods such as regression, A/B testing, and predictive modeling.

Insight: Non-normal data can distort model outputs and lead to misleading conclusions, especially in behavioral and user-driven contexts.

Impact: Helps teams identify hidden risks in analytics pipelines, improve model reliability, and make more accurate, data-driven decisions.

Status: Under review at General Review of Psychology.

Selected Projects

Power Analysis & Research Design
Monte Carlo simulation · survey methods · statistical modeling
Problem: Many applied studies lack sufficient data to support reliable conclusions, increasing the risk of false positives.

Approach: Built simulation frameworks to evaluate statistical power and design tradeoffs across research scenarios.

Insight: ~50% of mediation analyses in organizational research are underpowered, creating significant risk of misleading insights.

Impact: Enables teams to design more reliable studies, reduce false conclusions, and make better decisions with fewer wasted resources.

Status: Under review at PLOS ONE.
Workforce & Immigration Research
Qualitative research · interviews · global talent strategy
Problem: Immigration uncertainty creates hidden risks for employee retention, motivation, and long-term career planning.

Approach: Conducted in-depth interviews with employees navigating employment-based immigration processes to understand behavioral and organizational impacts.

Insight: Visa delays significantly affect employee commitment, perceived stability, and organizational trust.

Impact: Informs more inclusive talent strategies and helps organizations better support international employees while reducing attrition risk.
Cannabis & Leadership Experiments
Experimental design · behavioral science · leadership
Problem: Limited understanding of how substance use influences leadership communication and decision-making.

Approach: Designed and conducted controlled experiments evaluating the effects of cannabis on communication clarity, charisma, and judgment. Led end-to-end execution and mentored 20+ researchers.

Insight: Behavioral shifts under altered cognitive states can meaningfully impact leadership effectiveness, particularly in communication-heavy roles.

Impact: Provides evidence-based insights into leadership performance risks, informing workplace policy and leadership development.
Leadership & Sustainability (CSR / ESG)
Organizational behavior · sustainability · operations
Problem: Organizations invest in sustainability initiatives but lack clarity on how leadership behavior drives measurable outcomes.

Approach: Analyzed leadership practices within a large food manufacturing organization to assess their impact on waste reduction and operational efficiency.

Insight: Specific leadership behaviors are directly linked to improved sustainability performance, bridging the gap between strategy and execution.

Impact: Supports data-driven ESG initiatives by connecting leadership practices to measurable operational improvements.

Status: Under review at Journal of Operations Management.
