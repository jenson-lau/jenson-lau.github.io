Featured Project

Roles of Covariates in Mediation Models
Causal inference · simulation · experimental vs observational design
Built simulation-based analyses to examine how covariates affect statistical power and inference in mediation models. Identified a key methodological gap: assumptions from simple regression are often incorrectly extended to mediation models, leading to flawed study design. Found that covariates significantly affect power in observational survey studies, but have minimal impact in randomized controlled trials. Also demonstrated that variable type (continuous vs binary) alters power due to differences in measurement properties.

Impact: Helps researchers and organizations design more accurate, efficient, and decision-relevant studies.

Selected Projects

Power Analysis & Research Design
Monte Carlo simulation · survey methods · statistical modeling
Built simulation frameworks to evaluate statistical power and design tradeoffs in survey-based research. Identified that ~50% of mediation studies in organizational science are underpowered through a large-scale literature review. Developed sample size guidelines across model types to improve study reliability and reduce false conclusions.

Impact: Enables more accurate and efficient study design in applied research and organizational decision-making.
