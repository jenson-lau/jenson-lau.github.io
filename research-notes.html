Why underpowered mediation studies remain a problem
March 2026
Methodology Mediation Power Analysis

Many mediation studies still rely on sample size assumptions that are too optimistic. One reason is that researchers often inherit rules of thumb from simpler models without checking whether those assumptions hold in mediation settings.

My view is that power analysis should be treated as a design problem, not a box-checking exercise. The choice of model, variable type, and covariates can all materially affect the conclusions we draw.

A note on covariates in observational vs. experimental mediation models
March 2026
Causal Inference Simulation Study Design

Covariates do not behave the same way across study designs. In observational settings, they can meaningfully change power and inference. In randomized experiments, their role is often much smaller than researchers assume.

This matters because design choices are often justified using general advice from the literature, even when the design context is fundamentally different.
